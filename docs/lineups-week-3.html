Projected starting lineups

Rams at Broncos · Empower Field at Mile High. Offense and defense from published unofficial depth charts. Rams are 1–1 after MNF. Dobbins news is optimistic but unofficial; Harvey/Mims still TBD. Treat RB/WR rows as working projections until the midweek injury report.

Updated Tue Sep 22, 2026 · 7:50 AM MDT · ← Back to briefing · Week 2 lineups (archive)

Availability notes

  • Broncos RB room: J.K. Dobbins left Week 2 with a hamstring (10/36). Monday reporting (Pelissero via SI; CBS) is optimistic — “good,” hoping to play Week 3 — but the official injury report is not out yet. RJ Harvey (hamstring) was inactive Week 2. Jonah Coleman scored the go-ahead TD and sealed the clock (10/39, 1 TD). Still project Coleman as the working lead until midweek designations; Badie remains the pass-catch option.
  • Marvin Mims Jr. (foot): Inactive Week 2. Week 3 status TBD. If still out, project Troy Franklin / Pat Bryant / Lil’Jordan Humphrey behind Waddle and Sutton.
  • Broncos edge: Jonathon Cooper remains on the Commissioner’s Exempt List — depth chart lists Jonah Elliss as WLB with Dondrea Tillman behind him opposite Nik Bonitto.
  • Rams (now 1–1): Beat Giants 28–6 on MNF Sep 21 (Stafford 327/4 TD; Adams 8/195). Myles Garrett on IR after knee surgery (miss ≥4 games). Nacua missed MNF per FOX — Week 3 designation TBD/unavailable Tue AM. Stafford / Adams / Kyren Williams project as offensive core; add Nacua if cleared.
