Availability notes

  • Broncos RB room: J.K. Dobbins left Week 2 with a hamstring (10/36). RJ Harvey (hamstring) was inactive Week 2. Jonah Coleman scored the go-ahead TD and sealed the clock (10/39, 1 TD). Week 3 starter TBD — project Coleman as the working lead until midweek report; Badie remains the pass-catch option.
  • Marvin Mims Jr. (foot): Inactive Week 2. If still out, project Troy Franklin / Pat Bryant / Lil’Jordan Humphrey behind Waddle and Sutton.
  • Broncos edge: Jonathon Cooper remains on the Commissioner’s Exempt List — depth chart lists Jonah Elliss as WLB with Dondrea Tillman behind him opposite Nik Bonitto.
  • Rams: Myles Garrett on IR after knee surgery (miss ≥4 games per FOX Sep 21). Aaron Donald listed with Braden Fiske on the DE line on the unofficial chart. Stafford / Adams / Nacua / Kyren Williams project as offensive core. Rams play Giants on MNF Sep 21 before this game.
