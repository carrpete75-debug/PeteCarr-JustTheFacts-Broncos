Availability notes

  • Broncos secondary: Brandon Jones (hamstring) and CB Abrams-Draine (ankle) are OUT. Hufanga (ankle) is questionable after limited practices Thursday and Friday. Taylor Rapp was elevated Saturday (his third and final elevation) and is listed behind Jones at free safety on ESPN’s depth chart. Projection: Hufanga at SS if active (Key if not), Rapp at FS with Key/Skinner rotating. CB/KR Sean Fresch Jr. was promoted to the 53 and sits behind Moss at RCB on ESPN.
  • Broncos offense / front: Nix starts. ESPN lists Coleman ahead of Dobbins, with Schrader (elevated again) third. Pat Bryant was activated from IR Saturday and will play, per Payton. Waddle, Sutton and Allen have no designation. DL Uwazurike went on IR, so Roach starts in his spot, and Klis expects Jordan Jackson to be up on game day. OLB Tillman (hip/adductor, per the Gazette’s source) is questionable and “very uncertain.”
  • Panthers: Bryce Young has no designation. OLB Phillips, LB Okereke, LG Lewis and CB Lee III are all OUT. OL Corey Bullock (quad) and Torricelli Simpkins III (ankle) are questionable, per the Charlotte Observer. Carolina allows 140.7 rushing yards a game, third-most in the league.
