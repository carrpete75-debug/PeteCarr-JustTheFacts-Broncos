Week 8 · Sun Nov 1, 2026 · 2:25 PM MST · CBS

Projected starting lineups

Chiefs (5–0, at Seattle tonight) at Broncos (5–2) · Empower Field at Mile High. This is an early projection built from ESPN depth charts as of Sunday evening and adjusted for what happened in Week 7. Waddle (chest) and Allen (hamstring) left Sunday’s win late and are the key Broncos questions. Surtain is the possible returner. KC’s depth chart was pulled before its Sunday night game, and ESPN lists Rashee Rice out (hamstring) for that game.

Updated Sun Oct 25, 2026 · ~6:05 PM MDT (post-Week 7 · rail synced with home) · ← Back to briefing · Week 7 lineups (archive)

Availability notes

  • Broncos secondary: Surtain (ankle) missed his third straight game Sunday. Rapoport reported a “good chance” he returns against KC. If he doesn’t, Barron starts at LCB again, with Moss/Abrams-Draine and McMillian in the slot. Hufanga (ankle) was inactive again; Devon Key and Taylor Rapp cover SS.
  • Broncos offense / front: Nix starts. Coleman (9/54 in his return) joins Dobbins and Harvey. Waddle (chest) left late in Week 7; Mims (2/53 plus 103 return yards) is next at WR. Allen (hamstring) left late; Sai’vion Jones and Matt Henningsen back him up at LDE. Cooper started on his debut, opposite Bonitto.
  • Chiefs: Mahomes, Kenneth Walker III and Kelce headline ESPN’s depth chart. Rice is listed out for SNF (hamstring), so Worthy, Royals and Cyrus Allen are the WRs to watch. Monday’s update will pick up any SNF injuries.
