Availability notes

  • Broncos secondary: Surtain (ankle) missed his third straight game Sunday. Rapoport reported a “good chance” he returns against KC. If he doesn’t, Barron starts at LCB again, with Moss/Abrams-Draine and McMillian in the slot. Hufanga (ankle) was inactive again; Devon Key and Taylor Rapp cover SS.
  • Broncos offense / front: Nix starts. Coleman (9/54 in his return) joins Dobbins and Harvey. Waddle (chest) left late in Week 7, no update as of Monday morning; Mims (2/53 plus 103 return yards) is next at WR. Allen (hamstring) left late but said postgame he’ll “be fine” (PFT); Sai’vion Jones and Matt Henningsen back him up at LDE. Cooper started on his debut, opposite Bonitto.
  • Chiefs: Mahomes (19/33, 165, INT at SEA), Kenneth Walker III (14/36) and Kelce (4/44) headline ESPN’s depth chart. Rice (hamstring) missed his third straight game, so Worthy, Royals and Cyrus Allen are the WRs to watch. Starting RT Jaylon Moore is in the concussion protocol (AP). ESPN’s depth chart hasn’t been rechecked since SNF.
