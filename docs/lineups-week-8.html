Availability notes

  • Broncos secondary: Surtain (ankle) returns with no designation and is projected at LCB, with Barron back to a rotational role. Hufanga (ankle) is OUT for a third straight game; Taylor Rapp, promoted to the 53 Saturday after using all three elevations, has taken 69% of the snaps in his place (Denver Sports), with Devon Key also in the mix. FS Brandon Jones (hamstring) is questionable; if he sits, Rapp and Key are both on the field.
  • Broncos offense / front: Nix starts. Harvey (knee) went to IR Saturday, so Dobbins and Jonah Coleman split the work with elevated Cody Schrader as RB3. Waddle (chest) is questionable but trending toward playing (Schefter, Rapoport); Mims is next up, and Denver didn’t elevate a receiver. LT Bolles (hip) is questionable; rookie OL Gavin Ortega was elevated for interior depth. Allen (hamstring) is questionable; Sai’vion Jones would likely start if he sits (SI).
  • Chiefs: Mahomes, Kenneth Walker III and Kelce headline ESPN’s depth chart. Rice returns with no designation alongside Worthy and Royals; Kelce and RT Jaylon Moore are off the report. Saturday: CB L’Jarius Sneed to IR, rookie CB Mansoor Delane downgraded to OUT. The KC Star expects Kristian Fulton to start and Chris Roland-Wallace to see more snaps; CB Melvin Smith was signed to the 53. DE R Mason Thomas (personal) is traveling separately and expected to play; DE Tyreke Smith was elevated as insurance. DT Norman-Lott is out. Butker is expected to kick (CBS).
