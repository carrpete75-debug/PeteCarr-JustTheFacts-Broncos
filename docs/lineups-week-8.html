Week 8 · Sun Nov 1, 2026 · 2:25 PM MST · CBS

Projected starting lineups

Chiefs (5–1, lost 20–3 at Seattle on SNF) at Broncos (5–2, a half game back) · Empower Field at Mile High. This projection is built from ESPN depth charts and adjusted for Wednesday’s first official Week 8 practice reports. Surtain (ankle) was a full participant and is projected back at LCB. Waddle (chest) and Allen (hamstring) did not practice, so both are listed with their backups; Bolles (hip) and Mims (ankle) were limited. For KC, Rice was limited, RT Jaylon Moore was full after his concussion, and nickel L’Jarius Sneed (personal/knee) did not practice. Game designations come Friday.

Updated Thu Oct 29, 2026 · ~6:40 AM MDT (after Wednesday’s practice report · rail synced with home) · ← Back to briefing · Week 7 lineups (archive)

Availability notes

  • Broncos secondary: Surtain (ankle) was a full participant Wednesday, up from limited all of last week, and the Gazette says he’s “in position to return Sunday.” He’s projected at LCB, with Barron back to a rotational role. Hufanga (ankle) didn’t practice and wasn’t at the facility (Gazette), so Devon Key and Taylor Rapp should cover SS again.
  • Broncos offense / front: Nix starts. Coleman (9/54 in his return) joins Dobbins and Harvey. Waddle (chest) did not practice. Rapoport says he aggravated the earlier chest injury and his status depends on practice; Klis says “there is hope” and expects a questionable tag. If he sits, Mims moves up, though Mims (ankle) was limited Wednesday. LT Bolles (hip) was limited. Allen (hamstring) did not practice; Sai’vion Jones and Matt Henningsen back him up at LDE. Roach (neck) was full. Cooper started on his debut, opposite Bonitto.
  • Chiefs: Mahomes, Kenneth Walker III and Kelce headline ESPN’s depth chart, and Mahomes and Kelce (ankle, new to the report) were both full participants. Rice (hamstring/knee) was limited in his first practice since Week 4, so he’s projected back alongside Worthy and Royals. RT Jaylon Moore was full after his concussion; Joshua Ezeudu started for him at Seattle. Nickel L’Jarius Sneed (personal/knee) did not practice. Bolton (neck) was full per the Chiefs PR report and AP. KC signed Justin Tucker to the practice squad, but Butker is expected to kick Sunday (CBS). ESPN’s depth chart hasn’t been rechecked since SNF.
