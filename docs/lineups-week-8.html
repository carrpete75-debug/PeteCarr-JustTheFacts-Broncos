Projected starting lineups

Chiefs (5–1, lost 20–3 at Seattle on SNF) at Broncos (5–2, a half game back) · Empower Field at Mile High. This projection is built from ESPN depth charts and adjusted for Friday’s final Week 8 injury report. Surtain (ankle) has no designation and is projected back at LCB. RJ Harvey (knee) and Talanoa Hufanga (ankle) are OUT. Availability notes

  • Broncos secondary: Surtain (ankle) returns with no designation after three full practices and is projected at LCB, with Barron back to a rotational role. Hufanga (ankle) is OUT for a third straight game, so Devon Key starts at SS again. FS Brandon Jones (hamstring) is questionable; if he can’t go, Taylor Rapp, expected to be elevated a third time or signed (Gazette), steps in and Denver plays without both starting safeties.
  • Broncos offense / front: Nix starts. Harvey (knee) is OUT, so Dobbins and Jonah Coleman (9/54 in his return) split the work, and Cody Schrader is expected to be elevated as the third back (Gazette). Waddle (chest) is questionable after two limited days; Mims (full Thu–Fri, no designation) is next up. LT Bolles (hip) is questionable after three limited days. Allen (hamstring) is questionable; Sai’vion Jones would likely start if he sits (SI). Roach (neck) has no designation. Cooper started on his debut, opposite Bonitto.
  • Chiefs: Mahomes, Kenneth Walker III and Kelce headline ESPN’s depth chart. Kelce (ankle) went back to full Friday and has no designation. Rice (hamstring/knee) was full Friday, has no designation, and returns alongside Worthy and Royals. RT Jaylon Moore (concussion) returns. Rookie CB Mansoor Delane (shoulder) is questionable after DNPs Thursday and Friday; nickel L’Jarius Sneed (knee) and DE R Mason Thomas (personal; Reid says he’ll meet the team in Denver) are also questionable. DT Norman-Lott is out. Butker is expected to kick (CBS). ESPN’s depth chart hasn’t been rechecked since SNF.
