Availability notes

  • Broncos secondary: Surtain (ankle) was limited Wednesday and Thursday. ESPN projects him Questionable, and Broncos Wire, SI and Mile High Report still lean toward Week 8 vs KC for his return. Hufanga (ankle) has been DNP both days and isn’t expected to play; Taylor Rapp is likely elevated again. Moss (ribs) was full both days and expects a full game. If Surtain sits, Barron starts at LCB again with Abrams-Draine and McMillian.
  • Broncos offense / edge: Payton is calling plays and Nix starts. Coleman was full both days and is expected off IR by Saturday. Badie (thumb) was DNP both days and could be shelved. Waddle (chest) was limited both days, and SI says he’s in “little danger” of missing the game. Elliss (ankle) was DNP both days and is expected on IR by Saturday. Cooper is expected to play as a power rusher opposite Bonitto (Vance Joseph); his activation needs a roster spot if done by Saturday. Q. Robinson and Tillman are in the rotation.
  • Cardinals (2–4): Beat the Rams 18–16 last Sunday on six Chad Ryland field goals (0-for-5 in the red zone). On Thursday’s report, Love (ankle) was DNP for a fifth straight practice day, and CBS says he’s expected to miss. Baker, Nolen and Wingard were DNP again, and Melton (knee) and backup QB Beck (back) were downgraded to DNP. Garrett Williams was upgraded to limited, Jon Gaines II (knee) and Darius Robinson (back) were new limited entries, and Wills and Wydett Williams Jr. were full. Allgeier and Knight carry the backfield if Love sits. Depth-chart starters below are placeholders until Friday’s game statuses.
