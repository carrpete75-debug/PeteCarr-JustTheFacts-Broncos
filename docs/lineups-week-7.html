Availability notes

  • Broncos secondary: Surtain (ankle) is officially Questionable, but Ian Rapoport reports he’s not likely to play and has a “good chance” for Week 8 vs KC. Expect Barron at LCB again with Abrams-Draine and McMillian. Hufanga (ankle) is Out; Taylor Rapp and DB Sean Fresch Jr. were elevated Saturday. Moss (ribs) has no status.
  • Broncos offense / edge: Payton is calling plays and Nix starts. Coleman was activated off IR Saturday and rejoins the backfield; Badie (thumb) went to IR. Waddle (chest) has no status. Cooper was activated off the Commissioner’s Exempt list and is set for his season debut opposite Bonitto; Elliss (ankle) went to IR (eligible Week 12). Q. Robinson and Tillman stay in the rotation.
  • Cardinals (2–4): Out are Love (ankle), Nolen (ankle), Wingard (knee) and starting RG Jon Gaines II (Hayden Conner starts). Questionable: Baker, Melton, Darius Robinson and backup QB Beck. Saturday they promoted RB Evan Hull behind Allgeier and Knight, waived S Wydett Williams Jr., and elevated C Kingsley Eguakun (backup center) and DT Jared Harrison-Hunte; Cardinals Wire reads that as Baker likely playing and Robinson possibly not.
