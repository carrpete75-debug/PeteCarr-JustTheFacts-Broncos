Availability notes

  • Broncos secondary: Surtain (ankle) was limited Wednesday, his first practice since Week 4, and ESPN now projects him Questionable (SI: W8 vs KC still the likeliest target). Hufanga (ankle) did not practice and is Doubtful. Moss (ribs) was full after 20 snaps in Week 6. If Surtain sits, expect Barron, Abrams-Draine, McMillian, and Key/Rapp to carry the load again.
  • Broncos offense / edge: Payton is calling plays and Nix starts. Coleman was designated to return from IR and was full Wednesday. Badie (thumb) did not practice; Waddle (chest) was limited, and SI says he’s in “little danger” of missing the game. Elliss (ankle/leg) did not practice; Payton says it’s not season-ending, but he’s an IR candidate. Cooper practiced Wednesday and is expected to play, possibly on a pitch count; formal activation is due by Sat Oct 24. Q. Robinson and Tillman are in the rotation with Bonitto.
  • Cardinals (2–4): Beat the Rams 18–16 Sunday on six Chad Ryland field goals (0-for-5 in the red zone); Brissett went 24/35 for 218. On the Wednesday report, Love (ankle), DL Walter Nolen III (ankle), S Wingard (knee), CB Garrett Williams (knee), and Baker and Wills (illness) did not practice. Wydett Williams Jr. (concussion), Beck (back), and Melton (knee) were limited. Nobody has been ruled out. Allgeier and Knight carry the backfield if Love can’t go. Depth-chart starters below are placeholders until Friday’s game statuses.
