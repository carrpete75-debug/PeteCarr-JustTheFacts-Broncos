Availability notes

  • Broncos corners: Pat Surtain II hurt his right ankle on a non-contact play late in the 2nd quarter at SF. His Achilles is reported intact, with more testing pending. Riley Moss (ribs) also left in the first half. Jahdae Barron, Kris Abrams-Draine, and Ja’Quan McMillian finished the game. No diagnosis or timeline yet for either starter.
  • Broncos WR: Pat Bryant (ankle) was carted off in the 3rd quarter. If he can’t go, Troy Franklin and Marvin Mims Jr. are next on ESPN’s chart (Mims lost a fumble on a punt return at SF).
  • Broncos edge / RB: Dondrea Tillman (hamstring) missed Week 4. Jonathon Cooper remains on the Commissioner’s Exempt List. Jonah Coleman is on IR (earliest return Week 7). Dobbins / Harvey / Badie carry the backfield.
  • Chargers (0–4): Lost 30–23 at Seattle and finished with four rookies on the offensive line. Alt (neck), Slater (ankle), and Penning (head) all left. Awosika (fibula) is out and Pipkins is on IR, per Chargers.com. McConkey (foot) and CB Donte Jackson (hamstring) also left. Harbaugh: no updates Sunday.
