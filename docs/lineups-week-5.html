Week 5 · Sun Oct 11, 2026 · 2:05 PM MDT · CBS

Projected starting lineups

Broncos (2–2) at Chargers (0–4) · SoFi Stadium · Inglewood. Base lineups come from ESPN’s depth charts, with injury-driven projections after Friday’s final report. Payton is calling the offense again starting Sunday. Surtain (ankle) and Moss (ribs) are OUT, and Bryant (ankle) is on IR; WR Lil’Jordan Humphrey was signed to the 53. Projected: Barron and Abrams-Draine at corner, with Franklin / Mims at WR3. Chargers OUT: Alt, Penning, Awosika, James, D. Jackson, Q. Johnston, B. Thompson; McConkey questionable; Slater IR (ESPN).

Updated Fri Oct 9, 2026 · ~7:05 AM MDT · ← Back to briefing · Week 4 lineups (archive)

Availability notes

  • Broncos corners: Pat Surtain II (ankle) and Riley Moss (ribs) did not practice Wednesday. Asked about IR, Payton said, “I don’t think so, and if they were I wouldn’t say so.” ESPN’s Jeremy Fowler reported Wednesday that Denver is bracing for Surtain to miss two to four weeks, and Moss could miss a game or two. Projected: Jahdae Barron and Kris Abrams-Draine start outside, with Ja’Quan McMillian in the slot. Practice-squad CBs Brent Austin and Sean Fresch Jr. are elevation options. Final designations come Friday; Broncos Wire expects any IR decisions Saturday.
  • Broncos WR: Pat Bryant (ankle) was placed on IR Friday and will miss at least four games. Lil’Jordan Humphrey was signed from the practice squad. Troy Franklin and Marvin Mims Jr. are next on ESPN’s chart; Courtland Sutton came off the bench at SF.
  • Broncos edge / RB / TE: Dondrea Tillman (hamstring) returned to practice as a limited participant after missing Week 4. TE Adam Trautman (shoulder) was limited, Zach Allen had a rest day (limited), and RB Tyler Badie (foot) was full. Jonathon Cooper remains on the Commissioner’s Exempt List, and Jonah Coleman is on IR (earliest return Week 7).
  • Chargers (0–4): 18 players on Wednesday’s report. DNP: LT Rashawn Slater (high ankle sprain, IR candidate, no move yet), RT Joe Alt (neck), OL Trevor Penning (concussion), G Kayode Awosika (fibula), S Derwin James Jr. and CB Donte Jackson (hamstrings), WR Ladd McConkey (foot), OLB Khalil Mack (rest). Limited: QB Trey Lance, WR Quentin Johnston, CB Cam Hart, FB Alec Ingold, TE Charlie Kolar, WR Brenen Thompson, DL Dalvin Tomlinson, DB Deane Leonard, plus Branson Taylor and Scott Matlock (IR windows opened).
