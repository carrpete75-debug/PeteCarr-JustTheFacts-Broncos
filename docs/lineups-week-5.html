Projected starting lineups

Broncos (2–2) at Chargers (0–4) · SoFi Stadium · Inglewood. Offense and defense from ESPN’s current depth charts (unchanged Tue AM). Early-week projection: Denver’s first injury report is Wednesday. Updated Tue Oct 6, 2026 · ~6:55 AM MDT · ← Back to briefing · Week 4 lineups (archive)

Availability notes

  • Broncos corners: Monday MRIs brought relief, per sources (9NEWS, NFL Network): Pat Surtain II’s right ankle injury isn’t long-term, and ESPN’s Jeremy Fowler said he could miss “about two weeks.” Riley Moss (ribs) also isn’t long-term, return TBD. If both sit, Jahdae Barron and Kris Abrams-Draine start outside with Ja’Quan McMillian in the slot. Practice-squad CBs Brent Austin and Sean Fresch Jr. are elevation options. Payton won’t give updates until Wednesday’s report.
  • Broncos WR: Pat Bryant’s ankle sprain is expected to cost him “a few weeks” (Schefter). Troy Franklin and Marvin Mims Jr. are next on ESPN’s chart; Courtland Sutton came off the bench at SF.
  • Broncos edge / RB: Dondrea Tillman (hamstring) missed Week 4. Jonathon Cooper remains on the Commissioner’s Exempt List. Jonah Coleman is on IR (earliest return Week 7). Dobbins / Harvey / Badie carry the backfield.
  • Chargers (0–4): Harbaugh on Monday: LT Rashawn Slater has a high ankle sprain and is “more of a possibility for IR” (Rapoport: 4–6 weeks). RT Joe Alt (neck) is still being evaluated and could move to left tackle if he plays, with rookie Travis Burke at right tackle. LG Trevor Penning is in concussion protocol. S Derwin James Jr. and CB Donte Jackson (hamstrings) are week-to-week; WR Ladd McConkey (foot) is day-to-day.
