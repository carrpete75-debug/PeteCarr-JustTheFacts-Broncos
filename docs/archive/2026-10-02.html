Last game · Week 3 vs Rams · W 30–26

For the second straight week Denver trailed by double digits after the half and still walked out with a win — this time 30–26 under the lights, the first franchise instance of consecutive double-digit second-half comebacks. The Rams led 16–0 at the break on a Tyler Higbee 5-yard touchdown and three Harrison Mevis field goals (36, 35, 40). Denver had 67 net yards and a goose egg. Then the script flipped again.

Bo Nix threw a 5-yard score to Pat Bryant and hit Jaylen Waddle for the two-point conversion (16–8), then found Nate Adkins from 8 and Bryant again for two (16–16) before the third ended. Mevis’s fourth kick (48) made it 19–16. Talanoa Hufanga — after dropping two earlier INT chances — jumped Stafford’s third-down throw intended for Kyren Williams and went 66 yards for Denver’s first lead (23–19). Four plays later Stafford hit Konata Mumpfield for 48 when Riley Moss fell down (26–23). Nix then drove 65 yards in 10 plays (Bryant’s 39-yarder on 2nd-and-20, Troy Franklin’s 21-yarder, three Rams defensive penalties) and leaped for a 1-yard touchdown with 0:47 left. Stafford reached the Denver 14; after a false start, Hufanga tipped and intercepted the final end-zone heave.

Box score bluntness: Stafford 30-of-55 for 390, 2 TD, 2 INT (4 sacks); Adams 7/137 without Nacua; Rams 482 total yards and 26 first downs. Nix 17-of-34 for 186, 2 TD, 1 INT plus the rush TD (73.9 rating); Dobbins 17/49 (2.9 ypc); Waddle just 2/10; Denver only 257 total yards — but 3-for-3 in the red zone and a defensive touchdown. Hufanga finished with 2 INT (66-yd TD), a sack, 3 PD. Attendance 76,333.

What has to change before Levi’s: stop needing 16-point second-half miracles. First-half offense was dead (67 yards); the run game is still stuck (78 rush yards as a team). Good news into Friday: Waddle and Mims both went full again Thursday (second straight day), with Payton already saying Waddle is “doing fine” — both look available. The watch is OLB Dondrea Tillman (hamstring), who DNP’d a second straight day and remains Questionable with Friday designations still pending — edge depth is already thin with Cooper on the Commissioner’s Exempt List. The secondary cannot hang another 390-yard night on a healthy Brock Purdy / CMC / Kittle offense that is 3–0, even with Bosa out. Finish early downs and force San Francisco to play from behind — the comeback DNA is real; relying on it every week is not a plan.

Carry-forward: Payton on the slow starts — “we’re not splitting the atom here… we’ve got to get that corrected.” Undrafted rookie Kolbe Katsis’ 146 return yards were the most by a rookie in a Broncos debut (AP).
