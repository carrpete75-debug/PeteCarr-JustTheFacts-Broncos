Projected starting lineups

Raiders (4–5) at Broncos (7–2, first in the AFC West) · Empower Field at Mile High. Denver is on its Week 10 bye (Nov 15), so this is an early projection built from ESPN’s depth charts as of Monday, Nov 9, with the Week 9 injuries layered in: Waddle and Moss are in concussion protocol, Hufanga (ankle) and B. Updated Wed Nov 11, 2026 · ~7:00 AM MST (DraftKings line DEN −8.5 posted · Henley added at ILB · rail synced with home) · ← Back to briefing · Week 9 lineups (archive)

Availability notes

  • Broncos receivers: Jaylen Waddle left Week 9 in the first quarter with a concussion; the hope is he clears protocol by Nov 22 but there is no official timeline. Pat Bryant (4/56, TD in Week 9) is the next man up and keeps a role either way, with Sutton and Franklin also in the group.
  • Broncos secondary: Riley Moss (concussion) was ruled out at halftime Sunday, so Jahdae Barron filled in at corner. Hufanga missed four straight games with an ankle injury, and Brandon Jones (hamstring) and Kris Abrams-Draine (ankle) were out in Week 9. Taylor Rapp’s three practice-squad elevations are used up, so he would need a contract to play. The projection below assumes everyone is back; the Key/Skinner alternates are the fallback.
  • Broncos front / backfield: Uwazurike is on IR (earliest return Dec 13 at NYJ, a RookieRanks estimate), so Roach starts at RDE. RJ Harvey is IR-eligible to return Dec 6, and Elliss and Badie on Nov 27. Coleman led the backs in Week 9 (17 for 57, TD), with Dobbins behind him.
  • Raiders: ESPN lists Kirk Cousins at QB with Fernando Mendoza and Aidan O’Connell behind him. Las Vegas has not issued a Week 11 injury report, and ESPN’s roster tags are leftovers from Week 9, so none are shown here.
