Availability notes

  • Broncos receivers: Jaylen Waddle left Week 9 in the first quarter with a concussion; he was a full participant in Wednesday’s practice (Broncos.com), a sign he has cleared the protocol; no game status yet. Pat Bryant (4/56, TD in Week 9) is the next man up and keeps a role either way, with Sutton and Franklin also in the group.
  • Broncos secondary: Riley Moss (concussion) was ruled out at halftime in Week 9 (Jahdae Barron filled in) and was a full participant Wednesday. Hufanga (ankle, missed four straight) was also full; Brandon Jones (hamstring) and Kris Abrams-Draine (ankle) were limited Wednesday for the first time since Week 8. Taylor Rapp’s three practice-squad elevations are used up, so he would need a contract to play. The projection below assumes everyone is back; the Key/Skinner alternates are the fallback.
  • Broncos front / backfield: Uwazurike is on IR (earliest return Dec 13 at NYJ, a RookieRanks estimate), so Roach starts at RDE. RJ Harvey is IR-eligible to return Dec 6, and Elliss and Badie on Nov 27. Coleman led the backs in Week 9 (17 for 57, TD), with Dobbins behind him.
  • Raiders: Rookie Fernando Mendoza started Sunday’s 21–13 home loss to Seattle (17-of-29, 143 yards, sacked six times; ESPN box score), so ESPN’s depth chart listing Kirk Cousins at QB may be out of date; the starter for Nov 22 is not confirmed here. Brock Bowers (ankle/quadricep) did not practice Wednesday per the Raiders’ report; DT Tonka Hemingway (stinger) also DNP.
